📖 How Alerts Are Generated

ClassPulse uses simple, transparent rules — no machine learning or black-box models. Every alert can be traced back to a specific threshold:

Critical Attendance
Below 60% — immediate action required
Low Attendance
Below 75% — scheduled check-in needed
Failing Marks
Average below 33% — remedial sessions
Low Marks
Average 33–40% — concept review needed
Declining Performance
Recent scores dropped 10+ pp vs. earlier assessments
